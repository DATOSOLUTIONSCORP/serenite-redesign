About

Ador Mansat, DNP, FNP-C, MBA, BSChE

Ador Mansat, DNP, FNP-C, MBA, BSChE, is a distinguished and licensed Family Nurse Practitioner who is a straight A student and graduated with the highest honor (4.0 GPA) with a Doctor of Nursing Practice degree from the prestigious South University here in the US. "Ador" as he is fondly called, is renowned for his dedication to promoting health and wellness with an emphasis on longevity and vitality. With more than 18 years healthcare experience which includes ICU bedside clinical experience along with his 18 years chemical engineering, business management, and business ownership experience, Ador likes to be called “the BUILDER” and possesses the very rare combination of being an Engineer and a Primary Healthcare Practitioner. He is a life-long learner with multiple University and College degrees (a DNP, an MSN, an MBA, a BSN, a BSChE, an ADN, and an LVN degree). As “the BUILDER", Ador is a Healthcare Practitioner licensed in several states including California, and a life-long "Entrepreneur" whose passion is building businesses including a COVID-19 testing and treatment clinic.

About

Family Heroes and Remarkable Career

Ador together with his wife Bel, brother Arnold, son Matthew, and sister-in-law Romella, are all heralded as unsung heroes of the recent COVID-19 pandemic. They all risked their lives and health by personally going into many facilities to test, vaccinate, and treat our protected institutionalized population. Ador together with his close family and friends are part of the rare cadre of nurses and doctors who unselfishly risk their lives in service of their countrymen during the COVID pandemic. A Filipino-American with deeply-seated roots in the Philippines, Ador is a proud and top Chemical Engineering graduate of the Mapua Institute of Technology in the Philippines, known as Asia’s MIT.

In fact, Ador was a Professor at “Asia’s MIT” before he became a Board top-notcher and was recruited by several Fortune 500 companies and eventually headed the South East Asian operation of a big multinational company based here in the US. After retiring from his engineering and corporate jobs, Ador reinvented himself by studying in the US starting with a nursing professional degree and eventually culminating with the highest doctoral degree of his chosen healthcare profession. Being a life-long learner and an avid Mapuan, Ador continues to grow and evolve into a premiere longevity and wellness expert. Together with Dr. Heidi and his brother Arnold, Ador founded Serenite Wellness Medicine Medical Clinic.

With a deep-seated passion for helping individuals "Live Younger, Longer" (LYL), Ador has now become a pivotal figure in the field of wellness medicine. His recent personal achievement of losing 37 pounds himself underscores his commitment not only to his own health but also serves as an inspiring example of the effectiveness of his medical weight loss and management methods. As a scientific author, Ador has written a very popular, widely-implemented, and well-received DNP Capstone scientific paper on the novel combination of the GLP-1 semaglutide medication with Intermittent Fasting (IF) for weight loss and management. With the launch of his Serenite Wellness Medicine medical clinic mobile App, he is now scaling-up the basic premises of his Serenite Wellness Medicine medical clinic practice.

About

Innovative Success

As one of the founders and sitting at the helm of Serenite Wellness Medicine medical clinic's daily operation, Ador has developed and implemented a proprietary weight loss and management program, which has garnered significant attention for its innovative approach and remarkable success rates. This program is reflective of Ador's holistic view on health care, integrating the latest in medical science and engineering (particularly Artificial Intelligence, fondly referred to nowadays as AI) with personalized care to achieve optimal health outcomes. “High-Tech with High-Touch” is how Ador calls this novel approach to his medical clinic.

Expertise and Leadership

Ador's expertise and leadership in wellness medicine are grounded in a strong academic background, extensive clinical experience, and a fervent belief in the power of the latest medical science and engineering technology especially in proactive and preventative care. Through his work at Serenite Wellness Medicine medical clinic, he continues to make significant strides in assisting individuals to achieve their health goals, emphasizing sustainable lifestyle changes and proactive health management strategies. His approach is characterized by a compassionate and patient-centered philosophy, ensuring that every patient receives tailored care designed to meet their unique health needs. Ador's dedication to his profession and his patients is evident in his continuous pursuit of excellence and innovation in health and wellness. As a published scientific author, Ador’s Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) Capstone Project paper “The Combination Use Of Semaglutide And Intermittent Fasting For Weight Loss” can be found in the Journal of the Academy of Doctoral Prepared Nurses (JADPN). Here is the link: https://www.doctorsofnursingpractice.org/wp-content/uploads/project_form/abstract_140324075129.pdf.
