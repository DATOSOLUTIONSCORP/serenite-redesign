The Value Proposition of the LYL Serenite Corporate Wellness Program

Expert Guidance

Under the leadership of Dr. Heidi and Ador Mansat, DNP, FNP-C, MBA, BSChE, both highly esteemed in their fields, the program offers a dual approach to Living Younger Longer (LYL). Dr. Heidi focuses on aesthetic and rejuvenative strategies, while Ador Mansat, DNP, FNP-C, MBA, BSChE emphasizes longevity and health optimization.

Tailored Wellness Strategy

Our program is crafted to address both the aesthetic and health-related aspects of aging. We integrate the latest in medical technology, newest pharmaceuticals, natural nutraceuticals, lifestyle modifications, healthy gourmet nutrition, monitored physical activity, personal coaching, and mental health strategies to provide a holistic approach to wellness.

Corporate and Personal Benefits

Ideal for C-Level Officers, professionals, and business owners, this program is designed to enhance the productivity and wellbeing of key executives, ensuring that both individuals and companies thrive.

What's included

What’s Included in the LYL Serenite Corporate Wellness Program?

Before joining, ask the team to confirm the assessment tools, appointment locations and provider relationships. Optional treatments and discounted extras are separate from the program components listed as included.

  • Baseline Medical Exam (your choice of either a Telehealth or an In-person appointment)
  • Blood work (CBC, HgbA1c, Kidney Function Test, Liver Function Test, and Cholesterol / Lipid Panel)
  • AI-based medical 3D body scanning
  • AI-based 3D facial skin scanning
  • Discuss cardiovascular and cancer screening questions with a qualified clinician. Ask which assessments may be recommended, the tools used, their limitations and whether a separate referral or fee applies.
  • Download and use of the Serenite Wellness Medicine mobile App (works for both iPhone and Android phone)
  • Instructions on how to download and use a personalized disaster resource mobile App (iOS or Android)
  • Personal coaching using the Serenite Wellness Medicine mobile App
  • 8-weeks actual LYL challenge with personal coaching from Ador Mansat, DNP, FNP-C, MBA, BSChE and other coaches
  • Heart Rate monitor for the 8-weeks actual LYL challenge
  • Body Composition and Weight scale for the 8-weeks actual LYL challenge
  • Access to videos and resources for personalized exercise training, meal plan, and healthy recipes
  • Weight Loss and Management medical consultation (telehealth or in person) with an option of using the latest Pharmaceuticals for Weight Loss at discounted pricing
  • Facial (Facial Skin Rejuvenation)
  • Youthful Face and Skin medical consultation (telehealth or in person) with an option of using the latest Pharmaceuticals and Devices for Facial and Skin Rejuvenation including MORPHEUS8 and LUMECA by InMode and ATTIVA by Reveal Lasers at discounted pricing
  • Youthful Body Curves medical consultation (telehealth or in person) with an option of using the latest Pharmaceuticals and Devices for Body Sculpting / Contouring at discounted pricing
  • Sexual Rejuvenation medical consultation (telehealth or in person) with an option of using the latest Pharmaceuticals and Devices for Sexual Rejuvenation including BHRT (Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy), and Peptides at discounted pricing
  • Optional consultations about liposuction, plastic surgery or breast augmentation. Any procedure needs a separate treatment discussion. Ask who would provide it, where care would take place and which fees or discounts apply.
